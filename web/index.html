Send Wi-Fi to Tildagon

Fill in a network, open WiFi Manager on your device, select Recieve, then send it over Bluetooth.

This browser doesn't support Web Bluetooth. Use desktop Chrome, Edge or Vivaldi, over https:// or localhost.

Your Wi-Fi details never leave your browser except over Bluetooth — nothing is sent to this website or any server; the page just talks straight to your badge. That Bluetooth connection is unencrypted, though, so anyone in range could observe the details. It's meant for casual, share-with-friends networks, not secrets.

Need WiFi Manager? Sideload it via USB.
